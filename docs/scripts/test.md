excerpt: While the ultraviolet catastrophe is famously credited for the birth of quantum
  mechanics, the failure of classical physics to explain simple magnetism provided
  an equally vital, yet often overlooked, confirmation. This article explores the
  Bohr-Van Leeuwen theorem, which mathematically demonstrated that in a purely classical
  universe, magnets simply cannot exist. Discover how this fundamental paradox drove
  Niels Bohr to cement the foundations of the quantum revolution.
